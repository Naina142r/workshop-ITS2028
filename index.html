How to care for lettuce

☀️ Light

4–6 hours of sun daily. In hot weather, give it afternoon shade so it stays sweet.

🌡️ Temperature

Cool-season crop. Best at 45–75°F (7–24°C). Heat makes it bitter and sends it to seed (bolting).

💧 Water

Keep soil evenly moist, about 1 inch per week. Water at the base in the morning; shallow roots dry out fast.

🌱 Sowing

Sow seeds ¼" deep. They sprout in 7–10 days. Thin seedlings to 4–6" apart (closer for baby greens).

🪴 Soil & Pots

Loose, rich, well-draining soil, pH 6–7. Containers need drainage holes and 6"+ depth.

✂️ Harvest

Ready in 30–60 days. Cut outer leaves and it keeps growing ("cut-and-come-again"), or cut the whole head at the base.

🐛 Pests

Watch for slugs, aphids and rabbits. Use copper tape, a spray of water or row cover. Remove yellow leaves.

🔁 A Full Year

One lettuce lasts a couple of months, so re-sow every 3–4 weeks. Your "year" is a chain of harvests!
